Wiom Partner App · New booking · Design spec v1

Accept on the date screen, with a clock you can see

A new booking now shows how long the CSP has left to accept it, everywhere the booking appears. Acceptance moves from the address screen to the date screen, so a CSP only says yes after seeing both where and when. After the yes, the app goes straight to picking the team.

Date 26 Oct 2026 Design Saad Problem GROW-106 (Saurabh) Applies to CSPs on the new booking flow (pilot, about 60–70 CSPs)

01 · Why

Four in ten bookings get no clear decision

Of 558 bookings that reached the 61 pilot CSPs from 24 Sep to 1 Oct 2026, 242 (43%) got no explicit decision within 24 hours. Most of these bookings never got installed.

176timed out. The CSP never accepted, and the system took the booking back.
66accepted, but no technician assigned within 24 hours.
78.5%of these 242 bookings did not install within 14 days.

We read these as two gaps in the flow:

  • Before accepting there is a deadline, but the CSP can't see it. The booking is taken back if it isn't accepted in time. Today the countdown shows only on the home card, so a CSP who opens the booking does not see the clock.
  • Accepting and assigning are two separate jobs. Today the CSP accepts on the address screen, lands on the date screen, and has to tap "टीम को भेजें" separately. Many stop after the yes.

The goal is more bookings installed: a clear yes (with a team assigned) or a clear no, before the booking times out. The 24 hours is only the window the analysis used.

02 · What changes

Eleven changes, at a glance

1

Timer on every surface before acceptance new

The countdown shows only on the home card. The bubble and the booking screens show no time.
The same countdown shows as a ring on the bubble, a chip in the top bar of the address and date screens ("3:42:10 में स्वीकार करें"), and the card's second line on home. The ring stroke is itself a timer: it empties as the time runs out. Stacked bubbles each have their own ring.
2

Timer turns red in the last 30 minutes new

There is no urgency state.
When 30 minutes or less are left, the time is red on the home card and the chips, and the bubble ring turns red too, including on stacked bubbles. The text stays the same. At 0:00:00 the chip stays red.
3

Address screen opens with the timer first new

Bubble tap opens the booking card directly.
The screen opens blurred. The timer in the top bar grows into a large card, holds for 1 second, then shrinks back into place. The blur then clears and the map, address and CTA are usable.
4

Address screen has one way forward changed

"यह कनेक्शन लगा सकता हूँ" (accept) + "नहीं लगा सकता", and a separate "पता नहीं समझे ?" link.
There is one CTA: "इंस्टालेशन का दिन देखें" → date screen. The link becomes "पता समझ नहीं आया / नहीं लगा सकते?" and opens one sheet: call the customer, see nearby connections, or "इस पते पर नहीं लगा सकता" (cases 5A and 6). In the address card, the landmark row, the address and the voice note are 8 dp apart. The voice note is a new inline player (play button, waveform, duration, caller avatar in a rounded grey container).
5

Acceptance moves to the date screen changed

The date screen opens only after acceptance and has "टीम को भेजें".
The date screen is step 2 of 2, before acceptance. Its primary CTA is "यह कनेक्शन लगा सकता हूँ" (this is the accept). The secondary CTA is "इस दिन कनेक्शन नहीं लगा सकता". ← in the top bar goes back to the address screen (1/2). Nothing is sent and the timer keeps running. "दिन बदलें" works before accepting, the same as live, and the CSP still accepts afterwards. The "इस दिन कनेक्शन लगाना ही है" pill is removed.
6

After accepting: the commitment merges into the team list new

The CSP stays on the date screen and has to find "टीम को भेजें".
A success screen shows a tick and "सेवा स्थिति अच्छी बनाए रखने के लिए 18 जुलाई तक कनेक्शन लगाएँ" for 1.2 seconds. The line then moves up into a banner at the top of the team list, which opens on its own, and the team options appear a moment later. The commitment stays visible while the CSP picks who will install.
7

After assigning: success that becomes the drilldown changed

"{name} को भेज दिया गया है" screen with a "डिटेल देखें" button.
The success screen shows "सुनील यादव तय हो गए हैं" and "18 जुलाई तक कनेक्शन लगाएँ", then slides up into the drilldown (no tap needed). The drilldown title and subtitle are the same lines. If the CSP picks "मैं खुद करूँगा", the title is "यह कनेक्शन आप खुद लगाएँगे" (case 8). This applies everywhere a technician gets assigned.
8

Closing without accepting asks once changed

The dismiss sheet says "यह कनेक्शन अभी नहीं देखना?".
✕ on either screen opens "कनेक्शन अभी स्वीकार नहीं हुआ है", which explains how to accept. Primary: "बाद में देखूँगा". Secondary: the next step (see the date, or accept).
9

Timer info and time-up new

The bubble disappears silently at the deadline.
Tapping the chip explains the timer. If time runs out while a booking screen is open, the chip turns red at 0:00:00 and a "समय खत्म" sheet tells the CSP the booking is no longer theirs. "ठीक है" → home, with a closed card "कनेक्शन वापस ले लिया गया".
10

Customer reasons need a call first changed

Every reason shows to every CSP, in a fixed order per screen.
"कस्टमर ने मना कर दिया" and "कस्टमर से बात नहीं हो पाई" show only if the CSP has tapped a call to the customer at least once on this booking. On the date screen they move to the top when the CSP has called. See case 5.
11

₹750 line with a link to the scheme in every install drilldown new

The earning banner shows only on the new-booking card, before accepting.
ज़रूरी जानकारी in every install drilldown shows "इस कनेक्शन पर ₹750 कमाएँ". For eligible CSPs who have not opted in, a pink › next to it opens the scheme page (the same consent page as live). Opted-in CSPs see "इस कनेक्शन पर ₹750 मिलेंगे" with no arrow, and CSPs who are not eligible don't see the line. It is removed once the ticket is archived.

05 · Timer rules

One clock, shown in four places

  • What it counts: the time left until the booking is taken back if not accepted. The deadline comes from the server; today it is 6 hours from when the booking reaches the CSP. The app only displays the deadline and never sets it.
  • Format: h:mm:ss (for example 3:42:10). It ticks every second on the booking screens and the home card.
  • The ring is a timer too: the bubble's ring stroke shows the share of time left. It is full when the booking arrives and empties smoothly to nothing at the deadline. When several bookings are stacked, each bubble has its own ring, and the top one is visible in the collapsed stack.
  • Where it shows: the bubble ring, the address screen chip, the date screen chip, and the home card's second line ("3:42:10 · कनेक्शन स्वीकार करना बाकी है").
  • Last 30 minutes: the time turns red (text/critical) on the home card and both chips, and the bubble ring turns red (stacked bubbles too). Nothing else changes.
  • On accept: the timer stops and disappears from every surface. After acceptance no clock is shown (the 72-hour install window is not shown as a timer).
  • At zero with a booking screen open: the chip shows 0:00:00 in red and the "समय खत्म" sheet opens. "ठीक है" → home. If no booking screen is open, the bubble disappears, as today. In both cases the home card becomes a greyed, closed card ("कनेक्शन वापस ले लिया गया") and later moves to पुराने काम (archive), as today.
  • Chip tap: opens the timer info sheet ("3:42:10 में कनेक्शन स्वीकार करें").

06 · Motion

Three transitions

A · Timer intro (address only)

  1. Plays only when the address screen opens from the bubble (dissolve, 0.3 s).
  2. The address screen opens blurred, with the top bar and timer chip at their normal size.
  3. The top bar grows downward into a card near the top (inset 16 dp), and the chip grows with it (time text 12 → 19 sp), with "में स्वीकार करें" under it. It moves as one block, with a gentle spring, over about 1 s.
  4. It holds at the large size for 1 s.
  5. It shrinks back to the normal top bar with the same spring, over about 1 s.
  6. The blur clears (0.2 s), and the map, address card and CTA are usable.

B · Accepted → team list banner

  1. Tap "यह कनेक्शन लगा सकता हूँ" → accept call.
  2. On success, the screen shows a tick and "सेवा स्थिति अच्छी बनाए रखने के लिए 18 जुलाई तक कनेक्शन लगाएँ" (two lines).
  3. It holds for 1.2 s. There is no loader and no tap.
  4. The tick and line move up and shrink into a banner card at the top of the team list, over 0.7 s ease-in-out. The header and bottom CTA appear with it.
  5. After a 0.15 s pause, the team card ("इंस्टॉलेशन कौन करेगा?" + options) fades in, over 0.3 s ease-out.
  6. The banner stays on the team list, so the date and the quality line are visible while the CSP picks someone.

C · Assigned → drilldown

  1. Tap "टीम को भेजें" with a member selected.
  2. A full success screen shows a large tick, "{name} तय हो गए हैं" and "{date} तक कनेक्शन लगाएँ". For self-assign the title is "यह कनेक्शन आप खुद लगाएँगे". It holds for 1.2 s.
  3. The success block moves up into the drilldown's status banner over 0.7 s ease-in-out, and the rest of the drilldown fades in.
  4. This applies wherever a technician gets assigned: from the new flow, and from the drilldown's "टीम को भेजें".

07 · Cases

What happens when the CSP leaves the happy path

Case 1 · ✕ on the date screen before accepting

The CSP has seen the address and the date but closes without tapping "यह कनेक्शन लगा सकता हूँ". The booking is still theirs and the timer keeps running.

  1. ✕ → sheet "कनेक्शन अभी स्वीकार नहीं हुआ है". The secondary button accepts right away.
  2. बाद में देखूँगा → home. The card shows the timer: "3:42:10 · कनेक्शन स्वीकार करना बाकी है".
  3. Card tap → opens the date screen again, with the timer running. ← goes to the address screen.

Case 2 · Accepted, closed the team list without assigning

The CSP accepted (the booking is theirs and the timer is gone) but left the team list without picking anyone.

  1. ← on the team list → home. The card shows "टीम चुनना बाकी है" with an attention mark. There is no timer.
  2. Card tap → drilldown in the existing awaiting technician state: "टेक्निशियन चुनना बाकी है" with "टीम को भेजें".
  3. टीम को भेजें → team list → assign → success → drilldown (motion C).

Case 3 · The timer runs out while a booking screen is open

The deadline passes while the CSP is on the address or date screen. The booking is no longer theirs.

  1. The chip reaches 0:00:00 and turns red, and the "समय खत्म" sheet opens on top of whichever screen is open.
  2. ठीक है → home. The card is now a greyed, closed card, "कनेक्शन वापस ले लिया गया", and later moves to पुराने काम (archive), as today.

Case 4 · ✕ on the address screen

The CSP closes after seeing only the address. Nothing is accepted, and the timer keeps running.

  1. ✕ → sheet "कनेक्शन अभी स्वीकार नहीं हुआ है". Its secondary button is "इंस्टॉलेशन का दिन देखें" → date screen.
  2. बाद में देखूँगा → back where the CSP came from (home). The card shows the timer line, as in case 1. Card tap → address screen.

Case 5A · Can't install at this address (from the address screen)

The CSP taps "पता समझ नहीं आया / नहीं लगा सकते?". We first help them understand the address, and only then take a no.

  1. Sheet "पता समझ नहीं आ रहा?" has two help rows and a red text button: कस्टमर को कॉल करके पता समझें → call sheet (IVR code, as live) · इस जगह पर आपके पहले से लगे 4 कनेक्शन देखें → nearby map + list · इस पते पर नहीं लगा सकता → reasons.
  2. The reason sheet shows two reasons that fit the address: इस जगह तार नहीं जा पाता and नेट-बॉक्स नहीं है, plus और वजहें. Submit is disabled until one is picked.
  3. और वजहें adds कस्टमर ने मना कर दिया and कस्टमर से बात नहीं हो पाई only if the CSP has called the customer at least once on this booking, plus कोई और वजह (free text, always last and only in the full list). Without a call, the full list is the two address reasons plus कोई और वजह.
  4. Submit → "बुकिंग हटा दी गई है" with the chosen reason. For the cable reason it adds "अब इस जगह के आस पास बुकिंग आपको नहीं भेजी जाएगी". ठीक है → home, where the card reads "कनेक्शन आपने हटा दिया".

Case 5B · Can't install on this day (from the date screen)

The CSP taps "इस दिन कनेक्शन नहीं लगा सकता". We offer a date change before taking a no.

  1. Sheet "दिन बदल सकते हैं": दिन बदलें → the date-change flow · कनेक्शन नहीं लगाना → reasons.
  2. Changing the date before accepting works the same as the live date change (reason → date picker → "दिन बदल दिया गया है"). The CSP lands back on 2/2 with the new date and the timer still running, and still has to tap "यह कनेक्शन लगा सकता हूँ" to accept. The customer gets the existing date-change message. The same applies to "दिन बदलें" on the date screen itself.
  3. If the CSP has called the customer at least once on this booking: the two customer reasons are on top (कस्टमर ने मना कर दिया, कस्टमर से बात नहीं हो पाई), and the rest are collapsed under और वजहें: the cable reason, नेट-बॉक्स, then कोई और वजह last.
  4. If the CSP has not called: the customer reasons are not shown at all. The top two are the remaining ones (इस जगह तार नहीं जा पाता, नेट-बॉक्स नहीं है), and और वजहें holds only कोई और वजह.
  5. A "call" is any tap that starts a call to the customer on this booking: the call button on the date screen, or "कस्टमर को कॉल करके पता समझें" in the address help sheet.
  6. Submit → "बुकिंग हटा दी गई है" → home, as in 5A.

Case 6 · Address not understood

The CSP opens "पता समझ नहीं आया / नहीं लगा सकते?" only to understand the address, not to say no.

  1. The help sheet (as in 5A) → कस्टमर को कॉल करके पता समझें → the call sheet with the IVR code. This counts as a call for the reason rules.
  2. Or → इस जगह पर आपके पहले से लगे {n} कनेक्शन देखें → the nearby map + list. ठीक है → back to 1/2, with the timer still running.

Case 7 · Last 30 minutes

The booking is still unaccepted and 30 minutes or less are left. It behaves the same as the happy path, but the time is red everywhere, including the bubble ring (see Timer rules).

Case 8 · "मैं खुद करूँगा" (CSP installs it themselves)

On the team list the CSP picks themselves instead of a team member.

  1. Select मैं खुद करूँगा → टीम को भेजें becomes active.
  2. Success: "यह कनेक्शन आप खुद लगाएँगे" · "18 जुलाई तक कनेक्शन लगाएँ", held for 1.2 s, then it slides up into the drilldown (motion C).
  3. Drilldown: the same title, and कौन करेगा? shows आप खुद. ← → home.

10 · Measure

How we'll know it worked

  • Timed out before accept: share of bookings that reach the deadline without a decision (baseline 31.5%).
  • Accepted, no technician within 24 h: baseline 11.8% of bookings.
  • Time from booking to accept and from accept to assign, as medians.
  • Booking → install within 14 days: the outcome all of the above should move.
  • Guardrail: the explicit no-rate. A clear no is a good outcome, but a jump would mean the new flow pushes CSPs out.
